# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads own the quality strategy for the project, validating that work meets acceptance criteria and is safe for release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain test plans for features and releases
- Coordinate automated and manual testing across delivery cycles
- Validate acceptance criteria before release and during QA sign-off
- Report quality trends, defects, and risk to the project team
- Partner with developers and product managers to clarify expected behavior

### Goals
- Catch issues early and reduce production risk
- Improve release confidence and quality visibility
- Support a consistent definition of done across the team

### Typical Communication
- Daily or sprint-level quality updates in standups
- Release readiness reviews and bug triage discussions
- Test status summaries shared with PMs, developers, and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to reproduce defects and validate fixes
- Coordinates with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and release readiness
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing delivery risks and quality blockers

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Agile Coaches help the team work effectively, remove friction, and maintain healthy delivery rhythms.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help the team identify and remove blockers, dependencies, and process friction
- Coach teams on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track sprint health indicators and highlight delivery risks
- Support a psychologically safe, collaborative working environment

### Goals
- Improve team flow and delivery consistency
- Reduce unnecessary delays and distractions
- Strengthen team learning and continuous improvement

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of agile ceremonies and retrospectives
- Impediment tracking and escalation with PMs and stakeholders
- Sprint health updates and coaching conversations with team leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to align delivery rhythm and meeting cadence
- Partners with Product Managers to maintain a healthy backlog and prioritization process
- Supports Developers by reducing blockers and improving team operating habits

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders or Sponsors provide strategic direction, business context, and approval for investment in the project.

### Responsibilities
- Define strategic goals, priorities, and business outcomes
- Review milestone progress against agreed success metrics
- Approve scope, investment, and major decisions affecting the initiative
- Provide business context and escalation support when trade-offs are needed
- Act as a champion for the project across the organization

### Goals
- Ensure the initiative delivers business value and strategic alignment
- Support timely decision-making and risk management
- Maintain sponsorship for the project through execution and release

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or milestone-based stakeholder updates
- Decision and prioritization discussions with PMs and Product Managers
- Executive updates on outcomes, risks, and dependency needs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Aligns with Product Managers on strategic outcomes and roadmap priorities
- Works with Project Managers to review milestones, risks, and stakeholder communication
- Supports Developers and delivery teams by clarifying business intent and investment direction

---

## Tech Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads or Architects provide technical direction, uphold engineering standards, and help teams design scalable, maintainable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs, architecture choices, and major trade-offs
- Guide implementation decisions to reduce technical debt and risk
- Mentor developers and support engineering quality and consistency
- Identify dependencies, integration considerations, and design constraints
- Partner with release and operations stakeholders on reliability and scalability

### Goals
- Maintain technical quality and maintainability
- Reduce unnecessary complexity and execution risk
- Ensure the solution can scale with the business and product needs

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture decision discussions
- Code review guidance and technical trade-off conversations
- Planning alignment with engineering leads and PMs on dependencies

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to validate implementation approaches and technical decisions
- Supports Product Managers by clarifying feasibility, trade-offs, and technical constraints
- Helps Project Managers understand delivery dependencies and execution risks

---

## DevOps / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps or Release Engineers enable reliable deployment pipelines, environment readiness, and safe production releases.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Manage environment readiness, rollback plans, and release checklists
- Monitor production health and coordinate deployment verification
- Support incident response and recovery during release issues
- Document runbooks and operational procedures for repeatable execution

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, and repeatable software delivery
- Reduce release-related risk and downtime
- Maintain operational readiness for production changes

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness and deployment coordination with engineering and PMs
- Post-deploy validation updates and incident communication
- Documentation of operational steps and rollback guidance

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to ensure deployable, observable, and testable builds
- Coordinates with QA / Testing Leads on release validation gates
- Partners with Project Managers to align release windows and stakeholder communication

---

## Communications / Documentation Lead

### Role Summary
Communications or Documentation Leads maintain clarity across the project by ensuring information is documented, shared, and accessible to the right people.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain project documentation, status artifacts, and shared knowledge sources
- Coordinate stakeholder updates and communication rhythms across teams
- Capture decisions, lessons learned, and follow-up actions from meetings
- Support onboarding and knowledge sharing for new team members
- Ensure project materials remain current and easy to understand

### Goals
- Improve transparency and access to information
- Reduce knowledge silos and decision ambiguity
- Make project progress understandable for both internal and external audiences

### Typical Communication
- Progress summaries and status updates for stakeholders and leadership
- Documentation updates, meeting notes, and decision logs
- Knowledge-sharing materials and onboarding documentation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to keep project status and artifacts aligned
- Supports Product Managers and Sponsors with clear updates tied to outcomes and milestones
- Helps Developers and QA teams keep context, decisions, and release notes accessible

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles are intended to clarify accountability and improve cross-functional communication across the project lifecycle.
